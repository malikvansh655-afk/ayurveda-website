Small rituals.

Wellness does not need a dramatic restart. Give your body a few consistent signals of care, and let the rhythm build.

A softer start

Three rituals to carry
into your day.

01 · MORNING

Warm water

Keep a mug by your bed and sip warm water before reaching for your phone. Let your body wake before your inbox does.

02 · WEEKLY

Abhyanga

Warm a little sesame or coconut oil, massage slowly, and rinse after 10 minutes. Turn maintenance into presence.

03 · EVENING

Digital sunset

Dim the lights, put your phone away, and give your nervous system room to settle before sleep.

The best routine is the one you can return to with kindness.
